Privacy Policy

Clarity by Two Birds Innovation  |  Last updated: June 2026

Plain language summary: When you run a diagnostic, your answers are sent through a secure Two Birds Innovation proxy to generate your report. The proxy does not log or store your answers — they are used only to produce the result and then discarded. If you choose to enter an email address to receive your results, that email is sent to a third-party form service. Clarity has no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.

What Two Birds Innovation Does Not Collect

We do not collect, log, or store:

  • Your business name or diagnostic answers
  • Your SWOT analysis or recommendations
  • Any analytics or usage data

Clarity has no user accounts and sets no cookies.

What Is Stored on Your Device

Your browser's localStorage saves:

  • Your provider selection (and API key, if you choose to use your own)
  • An anonymous usage counter (number of diagnostics run, last completion date)

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's localStorage removes it completely.

How Your Diagnostic Answers Are Processed

Default (built-in AI): When you generate a diagnostic, your answers are sent from your browser to a secure Cloudflare Worker proxy operated by Two Birds Innovation. The proxy forwards your request to Anthropic (the AI provider) and returns the result. Your answers are used only to produce your report — they are not logged, stored, or used for any other purpose by Two Birds Innovation or Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes the request in transit as a sub-processor.

Optional (bring your own key): If you choose to use your own AI provider key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or Ollama), your answers are sent directly from your browser to that provider. They do not pass through our proxy. Your API key is stored only in your browser and is never sent to Two Birds Innovation. Review your provider's privacy policy:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Optional Email — Results Delivery

After generating your report, Clarity offers to email you a copy of your results. Providing your email address is entirely optional. If you submit your email, it is sent to Web3Forms (primary) or Formspree (fallback) — third-party form delivery services. Along with your email, we send: your business name, your readiness score, your top recommendation, and your suggested next step.

We use this information to send you one email with your diagnostic results. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you explicitly request it. Review the privacy policies of these services:

  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy
  • Formspree Privacy Policy

Fonts

Clarity uses the Atkinson Hyperlegible font (Braille Institute, SIL Open Font Licence). The font files are self-hosted — no request is made to Google Fonts or any external font service.

Cookies and Analytics

Clarity does not use cookies or any analytics service. We have no visibility into how you use the tool.

Children

Clarity is intended for business owners and adults making professional decisions. We do not knowingly collect information from people under 18.

Your Rights Under PIPEDA

PIPEDA (Canada's private-sector privacy law) gives you the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal information. Because we hold no personal data about you beyond optional email submissions, there is little for us to action. If you submitted your email and want it removed from our records, contact us at the address below and we will remove it promptly.
