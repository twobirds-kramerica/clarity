How it works

Six questions. One useful report.

The whole thing runs in your browser. One question at a time, no jargon, no trick questions.

  1. Answer six questions

    Plain questions about what your business does, how it runs, and where the friction is. If you can describe your week, you can answer them.

    About 3 minutes

  2. Clarity analyses your answers

    AI reviews your situation against Canadian industry benchmarks. Use the built-in engine, or bring your own AI provider if you prefer.

    15 to 30 seconds

  3. Get a report you can act on

    SWOT, priorities, quick wins, and a next step. Print it, save it, or bring it to your business partner or your bank.

    Yours to keep
