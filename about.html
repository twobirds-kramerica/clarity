About Clarity

Clarity is a free operations check-up for Canadian small and mid-size businesses, built by Two Birds Innovation. Six questions about how your business runs; one report with your strengths, your gaps, and which fix pays back first.

Why I built this

I spent 20+ years as a product manager at TELUS, Staples, and Start.ca. In that time, I watched the gap between large enterprises and small businesses grow wider with every technology wave.

When AI arrived, it was the same story. Big companies had dedicated teams, outside consultants, and technology budgets. Small businesses in Southwestern Ontario had YouTube videos and vendor pitches that turned confusion into expense.

I built Clarity because I wanted small business owners to have access to the same quality of strategic thinking that enterprise PMs apply internally, without needing an IT department, a consultant on retainer, or a subscription that auto-renews every month.

It runs on a secure proxy I built. Your business details are used only to generate your report and are never stored or shared. No account. No subscription. No lock-in. Just a clear picture of where your business leaks time and money, and what to do next.
