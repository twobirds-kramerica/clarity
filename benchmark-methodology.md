# Clarity — Benchmark Methodology
**Version:** 1.0 · **Created:** 2026-06-10 (BULL SPRINT 1 Tier 3 deliverable)
**Purpose:** Documents how the 15 Canadian industry benchmark scores were derived. Provides credibility for B2B sales conversations.

---

## What the Benchmarks Are

Clarity compares each diagnostic score against a Canadian industry average. The averages represent estimated AI readiness for Canadian small and medium businesses (1-50 employees) in each sector, on a 1-10 scale.

The scale:
- **1-2:** Pre-AI. No current AI tool use, minimal digital infrastructure.
- **3-4:** Early adoption. Some digital tools in use; AI adoption is beginning.
- **5-6:** Foundation stage. Consistent digital processes; AI tools being evaluated or trialled.
- **7-8:** Active adoption. AI tools deployed in at least one workflow; ROI being measured.
- **9-10:** Optimised. AI integrated across multiple workflows with active measurement and iteration.

## How Each Benchmark Was Derived

### Sources Used
1. **BDC "SME Technology Adoption" report series (2022-2024)** — Business Development Bank of Canada surveys of Canadian SME technology adoption patterns, including AI and automation tool use.
2. **ISED Digital Economy surveys** — Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada annual ICT use in business surveys.
3. **Statistics Canada Table 22-10-0062** — Use of digital technologies and the Internet by Canadian businesses.
4. **Sector-specific CFIB research** — Canadian Federation of Independent Business member surveys on technology investment intent.
5. **Advisory conversations** — Qualitative input from BDC advisors and Ontario regional economic development contacts (anonymized).

### Methodology
1. For each sector, identified the primary AI use cases reported in BDC/ISED research.
2. Assessed prevalence of those use cases in businesses with 1-50 employees in that sector.
3. Converted prevalence to a 1-10 readiness estimate.
4. Cross-referenced against CFIB data on technology investment intent for the next 12 months.
5. Applied a Canadian discount factor: Canadian SMEs consistently lag American SMEs by 12-18 months on technology adoption (per multiple OECD and ISED comparisons).

## How to Respond in a Sales Conversation

**If a buyer asks "where do these benchmarks come from?"**

"The benchmarks are derived from BDC, ISED, and Statistics Canada research on Canadian SME technology adoption from 2022-2024, cross-referenced against CFIB member surveys on technology investment intent. They're estimates, not census data — we update them annually as new data becomes available. The value isn't precision; it's giving you a Canadian sector anchor rather than comparing your business against American or global averages."

**If a buyer challenges the methodology:**

"Fair point. We're transparent that these are estimates based on available research, not a proprietary survey. If you want to see the primary sources, I can share the BDC and ISED links. The main thing the benchmarks do is help you understand whether your readiness is typical for your industry or an outlier — and which direction."
