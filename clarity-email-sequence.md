# Clarity — Post-Diagnostic Email Sequence
**Created:** 2026-06-10 (T3-5)
**Purpose:** 3-email sequence to keep leads warm after diagnostic completion and move toward a booking.
**Trigger:** Send when a prospect completes the Clarity diagnostic — during a live demo, after sharing the link, or when email collection is wired to the form.
**From:** the Two Birds Innovation sending address (see private vault for account details)

---

## When to Use This

The Clarity diagnostic currently requires the user's own API key. This sequence applies when:

1. **You run a live demo** with a prospect and walk them through their results — send Email 1 the same day, then 2 and 3 as scheduled.
2. **You share the link cold** (e.g., after a Chamber meeting) and they complete it on their own — ask them to share their score, then trigger the sequence.
3. **After Calendly booking** — use Email 1 as a pre-call warm-up if they've already run the diagnostic.

When the diagnostic adds email collection (planned for a future sprint), wire these to auto-send.

## Email 1 — Score Summary (send same day as completion)

**Subject:** Your Clarity AI readiness score — [score]/10

---

[First name],

Your Clarity diagnostic is complete. Here is your result.

**Your score: [X]/10**
**Canadian [industry] average: [Y]/10**

[One sentence characterising the result. Examples below — pick the right one.]

- *Above average:* "You're ahead of most [industry] businesses your size in Canada — a good foundation for moving quickly."
- *At average:* "Your score is in line with other [industry] businesses in Canada right now, which means you have a real window before this narrows."
- *Below average:* "Your score is below the Canadian average for [industry], which is worth understanding — it usually points to two or three specific things."

The full analysis is in your downloaded report. I'll follow up tomorrow with the one finding that stands out most.

Aaron

## Email 2 — Key Insight (send 24–48 hours after Email 1)

**Subject:** The one thing your Clarity score is pointing at

---

[First name],

Looking at your Clarity results from yesterday, the finding that stands out is [plain-language description of the primary gap area].

For most [industry] businesses at your stage, that gap is costing them time in [specific task or workflow — e.g., "preparing client proposals", "handling incoming inquiries", "documenting procedures"]. Not because the tools are bad — the tools work fine — but because the process around them is set up for a world without AI.

[One specific example of what a fix looks like:]

> [Concrete example: "A professional services firm in London changed how they handle initial client inquiries — from a 45-minute intake call to a structured AI-assisted intake form with a 15-minute review. Their response time went from two days to four hours. No new software; different workflow."]

I'm not suggesting you do that exact thing. I'm showing you the category of move that tends to be high-value at your score level.

If you want to talk through what it looks like for your specific situation, I have a 20-minute slot this week.

[Calendly link: calendly.com/twobirdsinnovation/30min]

Aaron

## Email 3 — Free Review Call (send 3–5 days after Email 1)

**Subject:** A 20-minute read on your AI readiness — no pitch

---

[First name],

Following up on your Clarity diagnostic. I have not heard back, which is fine — these things get buried.

Here is what I am offering: a 20-minute call where I walk through your results with you and give you my read on the one or two changes most worth your attention in the next 90 days. No agenda beyond that.

I do this because the diagnostic output is useful but it does not replace a direct conversation. The score tells you where you are. The call tells you what to do about it.

If the timing does not work: the offer stands. You can book any time at the link below, and I will have looked at your results before we talk.

[Calendly link: calendly.com/twobirdsinnovation/30min]

Aaron
